## 1. Executive Summary: Corrections Applied

All three advertising creatives have been overhauled and verified to ensure full legal, promotional, and advertising policy compliance:

1. **Shipping Disclosure Rectified:** Removed all instances of the false claim *"FREE SHIPPING On All Orders"*. Replaced with truthful, store-verified policy: **"FREE DELIVERY Above ₹999"** (Gold bold `FREE DELIVERY`, White bold `Above ₹999`).
2. **Medical Guarantee Removed:** In Creative B, retouched the gold circular seal from *"100% NATURAL & SAFE"* to **"100% NATURAL & PURE"**, eliminating the legally hazardous and Meta-disallowed "100% safe" medical claim.
3. **Unverified Social Proof Corrected:** In Creatives A and C, replaced the unverified claim *"Trusted by Thousands"* in the trust badge bar with the store-verified attribute **"Quality Assured"**.
4. **Subdued Medical Imaging:** In Creative C (Hindi), softened the glowing red ECG heart pulse wave graphic to prevent automated flags under Meta's *Shocking Content / Medical Imagery* policy.
5. **Multi-Format High-Resolution Export:** Exported all three creatives as:
   * **4:5 Feed Master (1080 × 1350 px)** — Optimal mobile Facebook & Instagram feed format.
   * **9:16 Story/Reels Master (1080 × 1920 px)** — Seamless vertical video/story adaptation with luxury botanical dark green safe zones (no text cut off).
