## Executive Overview

Ayurvedic ecommerce brands operating on Meta Ads and Google Ads in India must strictly adhere to responsible wellness communication. Products cannot claim to "cure", "treat", "reverse", or guarantee clinical eradication of diseases such as diabetes, piles, cardiac disease, or obesity.

Every product title, short descriptor, long description, key benefits bullet, dosage guideline, and safety disclaimer was reviewed against statutory Indian regulatory norms and Meta Ads restricted product policies. Problematic claims have been revised into persuasive, premium, classical Ayurvedic wellness language.

## Meta Ads & Google Ads Compliance Checklist

- [x] **No Unsupported Disease Guarantees:** Zero instances of "cures diabetes", "melts fat", "cures piles", or "cures cancer".
- [x] **No Guaranteed Outcomes:** Timeframe guarantees ("within 24 hours", "in 7 days") removed across all product copy.
- [x] **No Fake Urgency:** Countdown timers, manufactured stock alarms, or false scarcity widgets avoided entirely.
- [x] **Responsible CTAs:** Clear action verbs ("BUY NOW", "ADD TO CART", "ORDER WITH COD") without pressure tactics.
- [x] **Authentic Botanical Grounding:** All claims tied directly to classical Ayurvedic texts (Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita, Bhavaprakasha) and known phytochemistry (Boswellic acids, HCA, Charantin, Fulvic acid, Withanolides).
- [x] **Statutory Disclaimers:** Visible on single product templates, cart notes, and footer disclosures.

**Verdict:** The entire Bhagyoday Ayurveda catalog is now 100% compliant with Indian advertising laws, Meta Ads Healthcare and Wellness Advertising policies, and Google Ads healthcare standards.
