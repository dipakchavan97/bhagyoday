## 2. Image Processing & Asset Verification

1. **Zero Hotlinking:** No product images are served from CloudFront or third-party servers. All 21 images were downloaded, optimized, converted to WebP, and attached to WooCommerce products via the native WordPress Media Library (`wp-content/uploads/2026/09/`).
2. **Packaging Label Inspection:** High-resolution packaging labels were inspected to verify ingredients, dosage, Net Quantity (e.g. 30 vs 60 capsules, 500 ml bottles), and manufacturer certifications.
3. **Alt Text & SEO:** Descriptive, context-specific alt tags were assigned to every image attachment (e.g., *"Ashwagandha Shilajit Men Wellness Capsules 800mg (30 Capsules) featured bottle"*).
